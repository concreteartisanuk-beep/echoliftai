Questions & Answers

Straight Answers to Common Questions

Real questions from business owners before starting. If you have any other question, call Ian on 07494 867646.

Will my customers know they are talking to an AI?

Our AI voice uses ultra-realistic British conversational models that sound remarkably natural. It speaks with warmth, handles pauses, and answers questions politely. Callers appreciate getting an instant human-sounding answer instead of being dumped into voicemail.

What happens if a customer asks a difficult or unusual question?

If a caller asks something outside your configured factsheet (e.g., a complex custom architectural question), the AI handles it gracefully: "That's a specific question I'd like our senior specialist to review. Can I take your postcode and mobile number so they can ring you back within the hour?" It never guesses or makes things up.

How does call divert work with my mobile?

It's standard UK mobile functionality supported by EE, O2, Vodafone, Three, and all other networks. We provide you with a dedicated UK number and a 2-digit divert code (e.g. *61*). When you answer your phone, you speak as normal. When you don't answer within 3 rings, it diverts seamlessly to your AI receptionist.

How does the 100% Money-Back Guarantee work on the £19 pilot?

It's completely straightforward. If ApexVoice does not catch at least 1 genuine customer inquiry that you would have otherwise missed during your 7-day test, text or call Ian on 07494 867646 and we will refund your £19 back to your card immediately. Zero arguments.

Am I tied into any long contracts?

Never. The £19 pilot is a one-time fee with zero recurring billing. If you decide to stay on our monthly plan (£99/mo), it is a rolling 30-day subscription that you can pause or cancel at any time with a single click in your portal.

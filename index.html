Straight answers

How long until I get it?

Writing starts the second your payment clears and normally finishes in a few minutes. You land straight on your report page, and the link is emailed to you as well so you can close the tab and come back to it.

Is it really written for my business, or is it a template with my name swapped in?

It's generated from the brief you fill in — your industry, your city, your USP. The keyword plan, competitor breakdown and 90-day plan are all built around those specifics, which is why we ask for them before taking payment rather than after. Two businesses in different towns get genuinely different reports.

Do I have to get on a sales call?

No. There's no call, no onboarding session and nothing to schedule. You buy the report, you get the report. If you later want help executing it, a demo is there when you want it — but the report stands on its own and is yours to act on with or without us.

Is £47 a subscription or a trial that starts billing me?

Neither. It's a single one-off charge for one document. Nothing recurring is set up, there's no card on file for future billing, and there's nothing to cancel.

What format is it, and can I share it?

It's a branded, print-ready web report of around ten pages with a one-click Download PDF button. It's yours — share it with your team, your accountant or your marketing agency however you like.

What happens to my details?

Your brief is used to write your report and to email it to you. Card details go directly to Stripe and never reach our servers. We won't pass your details to anyone else.

What if something goes wrong with the payment or delivery?

Your order is recorded against your payment, so nothing is lost — reopening the link always returns the same report rather than charging you again. If it doesn't arrive, email info@echoliftai.co.uk with the reference on your receipt and we'll sort it personally.
